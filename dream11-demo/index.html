Surface A · the proven bet

Private-league sponsorships

Pick a brand. The engine scores every private league on brand-fit, bundles the best into a sellable cohort, and auto-builds the sponsorship creative — work a person would otherwise do by hand, league by league.

Step 1 · choose a brand to match

Demo population: scored leagues

Step 2 · league scoring engine

Ranked by brand-fit. Each score breaks down into the four signals the engine weighs — affinity · engagement · size · region. Cold-start leagues (<2 weeks of chat) are flagged and discounted.

Step 3 · the sellable cohort

—

Matching leagues are bundled into one brand-fit cohort (a package sold for a season). Up to ~500 similar leagues per package.

Step 4 · auto-generated creative

Generated per brand per cohort — league skin, chat stickers, matchday graphic. A human reviews for brand safety before anything goes live.

Step 5 · opt-in consent

Nothing changes in a league without the admin's consent. New consent is captured here — no legacy data is used.

Step 6 · brand performance dashboard

What the sponsoring brand sees once the campaign is live.

Go live to populate the brand's live dashboard.

Where it breaks (flagged, not hidden): brand-new leagues can't be scored for ~2 weeks until chat history builds. Scoring thousands of leagues at once on the busiest match nights is the one real engineering challenge.

Open questions to validate in the room

  1. How many real active private leagues exist, and typical size? Replaces the biggest assumption in Surface A.
  2. How many advertisers are on Dream11's own in-app inventory (not IPL-wide)? Tightens Surface B.
